FAQ

Questions, answered.

The things distributors and venues ask us most. Can't see yours?

Ask us anything
Do you supply venues directly, or only through distributors?

Both. We deliver fresh, direct to venues in Sydney, Canberra, Wollongong and the Southern Highlands. Everywhere else, our frozen range is stocked by foodservice distributors including PFD, Superior, Bidfood and The Provedores.

When can I get fresh deliveries?

Any days from Monday to Saturday. Order by 2pm and we'll deliver on your next delivery day.

How long do fresh buns last?

Five days from baking.

What's the minimum order for the frozen range?

Two pallets, which is 96 cartons or 2,880 buns. Each carton holds 30 buns, and each pallet holds 48 cartons.

How long does a frozen order take?

Two to three weeks from order to dispatch.

How long do frozen buns last, and how do I thaw them?

Six months at −18°C. Thaw them overnight in the fridge and they're ready to serve.

Do the buns come sliced?

Yes. Our buns are sliced for your convenience, ready to toast and serve.

Can I try samples before ordering?

Yes. Distributors get a frozen sample carton of each bun, and venues get a sample box. Tell us what you're making and we'll help you pick.

Do you have gluten free and vegan options?

Yes. Our gluten free & vegan bun covers coeliac and plant-based guests, and it also comes in slider size.

Can you do custom sizes or private label?

Yes. Ask us about custom sizes, toppings or your own branding.

Can I get product specs and allergen information?

Yes. We have product specs, allergen and nutrition information ready for your catalogue and customers, and we're a HACCP-certified bakery. Ask us and we'll send them through.
