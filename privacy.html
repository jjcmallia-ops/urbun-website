Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Urbun Bakery is a trading name of Mallia Industries Pty Ltd (ABN 25 617 284 705), referred to here as "Urbun", "we", "us" or "our". We respect your privacy and handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles.

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and how you can access or correct it or make a complaint.

What we collect

We're a wholesale bakery, so most of the information we hold is about the people we do business with. Depending on how you deal with us, we may collect:

  • Contact details: your name, business name, email address and phone number.
  • Enquiry details: what you tell us in our enquiry form or when you contact us, such as the type of business, expected volumes, storage, the states you supply and the buns you're interested in.
  • Account and ordering details: delivery addresses, order history, invoicing and payment details for trade accounts.
  • Job application details: your CV, work history and anything else you send us when applying for a role.
  • Website data: technical and usage information such as your IP address, device and browser type, the pages you visit and how you found our site. Our website host logs some of this automatically, and we collect more using cookies and analytics tools (see below).

We don't intentionally collect sensitive information (such as health information) unless it's reasonably needed, for example for a job application, and you've agreed to it.

How we collect it

We collect personal information directly from you when you fill in our enquiry form, email or call us, place orders, open a trade account or apply for a job. Sometimes we receive information from third parties, such as a distributor or venue that refers you to us, or a referee you've nominated.

You don't have to give us your personal information, but if you don't, we may not be able to answer your enquiry, supply you or consider your application.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and send you pricing, samples or product information you've asked for.
  • To set up and manage trade accounts, process and deliver orders, and send invoices.
  • To provide customer service and keep in touch about your account and our products.
  • To assess job applications.
  • To run, maintain and improve our website and business.
  • To meet our legal, food safety and record-keeping obligations.

If we send you marketing emails, you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in the email or by contacting us.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information. We only share it where needed for the purposes above, including with:

  • service providers who help us run our business, such as website hosting, email, accounting and ordering software, and delivery providers;
  • distributors, where you've asked to buy Urbun products through one;
  • our professional advisers, such as accountants and lawyers;
  • government bodies or others where required or authorised by law.

Overseas storage

Some of our service providers, such as website hosting, email and cloud software providers, may store or process information outside Australia, including in the United States. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps to make sure those providers handle your information in a way that's consistent with the Australian Privacy Principles.

Cookies and website data

We use Google Tag Manager to run analytics and measurement tools on our website, such as Google Analytics. These tools use cookies and similar technologies to collect information like the pages you visit, how long you stay, the device and browser you use, your approximate location and how you arrived at our site. We use this to understand how people use our website and to improve it. Google may process this information in the United States.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on. To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see Google's partner sites policy.

Our pages also load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with Google. Links to our Instagram and Facebook pages take you to those platforms, which have their own privacy policies.

How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, change or disclosure. This includes using reputable service providers, limiting access to staff who need it, and using password-protected systems. When we no longer need personal information, we take reasonable steps to destroy or de-identify it.

Access and correction

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it if it's wrong, out of date or incomplete. Contact us using the details below. We'll respond within a reasonable time, usually within 30 days. If we can't give you access, we'll explain why.

Complaints

If you think we've mishandled your personal information, please contact us first so we can look into it. We'll aim to respond within 30 days.

If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.
